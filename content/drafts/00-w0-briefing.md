## A · start
Ground rules while it's quiet. Nothing in this building is owed to you — not a meeting, not a slot, not a microphone. Each week something happens, and the week is whatever that opens: a chair with fifteen minutes, a room worth packing, a door left ajar. When it opens, you get ONE real move — spend it or lose it. And what you spend your weeks doing, you get better at doing. Weeks disappear faster than you think. Lateral, crossover, conference, gone.

- **Procedure:** No standing appointments. Openings only — and openings expire Friday. Finally, a calendar as honest as the real one.
- **Talk Story:** Then read every room fast. The week that opens a door rarely announces which one.
