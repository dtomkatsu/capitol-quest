# ev-emergency

## A · start
Health department's in the hallway with a real one: a federal reimbursement fell through and a rural clinic network needs a $40M emergency appropriation to stay open through the fiscal year. The rescue coalition wants your campaign's name on the joint letter by Friday.

- **Base Building:** Forty million to keep the doors open in places with one doctor per island district. The network that shows up for you in March is built on days like this.
- **Policy Brain:** Every emergency signed off-cycle is a line item nobody voted on in the open. Ask where the forty comes FROM before you sign.

### 1. Sign the letter. Clinics stay open.
Your campaign's name goes second on the list. The clinic director cries in the hallway. The fiscal note does not.

- effect: -$40M revenue, +4 support
- then: end

### 2. Ask for a scrubbed budget first. Defer.
Prudent, defensible — and the neighbor-island papers run 'LAWMAKERS STALL CLINIC RESCUE' anyway.

- effect: -3 support, +3 influence
- then: end
