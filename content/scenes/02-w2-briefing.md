# w2-briefing

## A · start
You enter the cramped room, to an audience of around 20 people. Rep. Silva is the only legislator present—the rest sent their junior staff.

- **Educator:** A billion-dollar number lands on a junior staffer as a headline, not a sentence. Give them one they can carry upstairs.
- **Talk Story:** Silva is the only one in the room who can vote. The other nineteen are here to tell their bosses what you said.

### 1. [Policy Brain] "We're staring down a revenue shortfall of $1 billion. We need to make up for this by raising taxes on the wealthy, like with capital gains..."
Pens move. Silva nods once, slowly, the way a member does when she has just been handed a number she can repeat. Somewhere in the back, a staffer writes "$1B" and underlines it twice.

- effect: +3 influence
- then: end

### 2. [Mobilizing] "It's harder than ever for local families to make ends meet..."
The room changes. Phones come down, and a staffer near the door starts nodding at her own kitchen table. Silva looks up from her notes. This is the version of the speech her district will hear.

- effect: +3 support
- then: end
