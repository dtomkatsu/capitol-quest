# ev-introducer

## A · start
**Rep. Christina Silva:** She catches you before the elevator, holding a marked-up copy of your own bill. "Leadership's office called. Not about your bill — about me." A committee assignment, a district project, a favour owed; the specifics don't matter and she doesn't offer them. "They'd like to know if I'm carrying this all the way or just filing it. I have to give them an answer this week, and whatever I say, I have to live in this building afterward."

- **Procedure:** This is the pressure that never appears in a journal entry. No vote, no record — just a member deciding how much of her own year to spend on you.
- **Talk Story:** She came to you first. That is the whole tell. Somebody who had already folded would have sent an email on Friday.
- **Base Building:** If the answer is 'you're on your own,' you don't get a second one of these. Members talk to each other more than they talk to us.

### 1. "Tell them you're carrying it. We'll put people in your district behind you."
She exhales. "Then I'll say yes." You have just promised turnout in a district you do not organize yet, which is a problem for a future week and a real one.

- effect: +10 house, +2 support, -3 influence
- then: end

### 2. "Say whatever keeps you safe. I'd rather have you in April."
"...Thank you." Nobody in this hallway says that to a member, and it costs the bill something this week — but she will still be picking up the phone in April, which is more than most advocates can say.

- effect: +4 house, +2 influence
- then: end

### 3. [Procedure 13 red] "Ask them to put that request in writing."
- mods: influence/25, TALK STORY/4
Pass:
Silva actually laughs. "They will never do that." Which is the point, and she sees it a beat later: a request nobody will write down is a request nobody can enforce. The call is not returned, and the bill keeps its number.

- effect: +8 house, +3 influence, flag nothing-in-writing
Fail:
"That's how you talk to an agency, not to the people who set my committee." She puts the marked-up copy back in her bag. "I'll handle it my way." You do not learn what their way was.

- effect: -8 house, -2 influence
