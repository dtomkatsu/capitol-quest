# w4-souza

## A · start
Money Committee Week. Ways and Means posts its notices Tuesday at four, and by twenty past, every advocate in the building has read them off a phone in the same hallway. You have called Souza's office six times. His scheduler is unfailingly warm, remembers your name without being told, and has nothing — not this week, not next, and the week after that is holding for the chair. Room 211 is the same koa as every other door on the floor. It is closed.

- **Procedure:** Ways and Means is the last gate before conference and its deadline is week eleven. You have time. What you don't have is a way in.
- **Talk Story:** Six calls and she still says your name warmly. That's a scheduler protecting a chair, not one brushing off a stranger — and the difference is worth something.
- **Base Building:** There are twelve other members on that committee, and not one of them has a scheduler holding weeks. A chair is not the only person in a room.

### 1. Take whatever she offers. Any slot, any hour.
7:40 on a Friday morning, three weeks out, fifteen minutes, subject to the chair's floor schedule. You take it and you write it in pen.

- effect: +2 influence
- then: end

### 2. Stop chasing the chair. Work the other twelve members.
Four offices in a day, two of them freshmen nobody has ever asked for anything. Thursday, a WAM member you have never met asks a question in a hearing that came straight off your one-pager.

- effect: +3 support, -1 influence
- then: end
