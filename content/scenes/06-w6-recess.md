# w6-recess

## A · start
The building empties on Thursday and does not fill again until the following Wednesday.

Nobody voted for this. The constitution requires it — at least five days of recess, somewhere between the twentieth and the fortieth legislative day — and the two chambers pick the actual dates between themselves by concurrent resolution. Sixty legislative days in a regular session. Five of them are gone by design, and the weekends and holidays around them were never counted in the sixty to begin with.

What that means for you is arithmetic, and it is not friendly. No notices post. No committee meets. No chair schedules anything. And not one deadline in front of you moves by a single day.

- **Procedure:** Read it exactly: the recess suspends the WORK, not the clock. Every deadline you are running at is still where it was on Wednesday, with one fewer week to reach it.
- **Policy Brain:** Sixty legislative days to move a budget the size of a small country's. This is the week to notice how few that actually is.
- **Base Building:** Fifty-one members just went home to their districts. For five days the people who are hardest to reach in Honolulu are sitting in a folding chair at a community meeting on their own island.
- **Talk Story:** Everyone you have been chasing is unreachable and so is everyone chasing you. That cuts both ways, and it will not happen again this session.

### 1. Go home. Sleep. Be at the Saturday cleanup like a person.
You pull weeds at the stream cleanup for four hours beside people who have been getting your emails since January and had never once seen your face on a Saturday. Nobody talks about the bill. Everybody notices you came.

- effect: +3 support
- then: end

### 2. [Base Building 12 white] District week: work the neighbor-island offices while members are home.
- mods: support/30, MOBILIZING/3
Pass:
Three islands in four days on the cheapest flights that exist. You catch a WAM member at her own district office with no scheduler between you, in slippers, at four in the afternoon — fifty minutes, no gatekeeper, and a question at the end that means she read it. Room 211 could not have bought you that.

- effect: +8 senate, +3 support, unlock talk sen-x=talk-sen
Fail:
Two locked doors, one staffer covering three offices, and a member who is genuinely, visibly on vacation with her grandchildren and does not want to be your Tuesday. You apologize and mean it. The flights were not cheap.

- effect: -1 influence, +1 support

### 3. [Policy Brain 12 white] Work the recess like it isn't one. Draft the Ways and Means amendment now.
- mods: PROCEDURE/3
Pass:
Five days, no phone, no hallway. You come back with the Senate draft finished and a fiscal note that answers the question Souza actually asks — not what it raises, what it costs him in a bad year. It is sitting in a drawer, ready, weeks before anyone will ask for it.

- effect: +3 influence, flag recess-draft
Fail:
You spend five days alone with a document and produce eleven pages nobody asked for. On Wednesday the building comes back rested and you come back with a draft and a headache.

- effect: +1 influence, -2 support

### 4. Put the coalition on planes. Five days of rooms nobody is watching.
Four islands, eleven rooms, no press and no member in any of them. The organizing that shows up in April is built in weeks exactly like this one, which is why it is invisible in March.

- effect: +6 support, -2 influence
- then: B
