# w1-grange

## A · start
**Curtis Grange:** Grange finds you by the koa doors, all smiles. "Big session, yeah? Our members can live with a lot — but the conveyance thing reads like a tax on selling your own house. Help me help you: carve out everything under three million, we stay neutral. Testify friendly, even."

- **Policy Brain:** The bill already exempts owner-occupied homes under the standard threshold — that's not spin, that's the bill text. Cite it and watch him improvise.
- **Base Building:** Neutral-to-friendly from the roundtable is a real asset. Just don't pay for it with language the base will notice missing.

### 1. "It already exempts owner-occupied homes. Read the bill, Curtis."
The smile holds, one degree cooler. "Then our testimony will read the bill very... closely."

- effect: +2 support, -2 influence
- then: end

### 2. "Send me your carve-out language. No promises."
"That's all I ask." It is never all he asks.

- effect: +3 influence
- then: end

### 3. [Base Building 12 red] "Say it at the hearing, then. On the record, under your own name."
- mods: support/25
Pass:
The smile finally goes. "We don't usually testify on the record this early." They don't testify at all. Your bill gets a hearing without a villain in the room.

- effect: +3 support, -2 influence
Fail:
"Happy to!" And he does — warmly, at length, in favor of a version of your bill that does not exist. The committee writes down HIS number.

- effect: +2 influence, -4 support
