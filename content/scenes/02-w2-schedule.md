# w2-schedule

## A · start
Kawaguchi's office is two doors down from the briefing room, and it has a gatekeeper: an office manager with a paper calendar, a phone cradled on her shoulder, and the patience of someone who has said "he's in committee" four hundred times today. Everyone in the hallway wants fifteen minutes with the chair of Finance.

- **Procedure:** The chair's calendar is the real referral list. Whoever gets onto it first is not always whoever he wants to hear.
- **Talk Story:** She is not the obstacle. She decides whether he hears your name as a favor or as a nuisance.

### 1. [Talk Story 3+] [Talk Story 9 white] Introduce yourself to the office manager.
- mods: influence/25
Pass:
She puts the phone down, which seems to surprise her too. "What was your name again?" You tell her. "He has fifteen minutes next week, before the lateral file lands. Don't be late, and don't bring a binder." She writes your name in pen.

- effect: +2 influence, flag kawaguchi-date
- then: end
Fail:
"He's in committee." She writes your name on a sticky note and presses it to the edge of the desk. "We'll call you." The note will be there next week. So will the line.

- effect: -2 influence
- then: end

### 2. Leave a fact sheet and your card. Don't take up her time.
She slides it into a tray that is already full. It is, at least, a very polite way to be forgotten.

- effect: +1 support
- then: end
